# 内录 App 图标

生成方式：Codex 内置 imagegen 工具，非 CLI，无参考图。源文件 `AppIcon-source.png`；构建时仅用 sips 机械缩放为苹果要求的 1024×1024，再交给 Asset Catalog 编译。

最终提示词：

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished iOS app icon image for an internal-audio recording app. Deliver a square 1024 by 1024 opaque PNG, edge-to-edge background with no outer margin and no pre-rounded app tile corners (iOS will mask it). Elegant minimal design: a luminous aqua/turquoise waveform with five rounded vertical bars, integrated into a subtle circular recording ring, centered and very legible at small sizes. Deep midnight navy background with a restrained smooth teal glow behind the mark. Carefully balanced geometry, premium modern native iPhone utility aesthetic, subtle dimensional light, crisp edges, high contrast. No text, no letters, no microphone, no phone mockup, no screenshots, no watermark, no decorative clutter. One single app icon, not a contact sheet.
